User Manual & Instructions

Getting Started

Welcome to CallMee! To join a meeting, simply enter your Display Name and the Room ID. If the room has a password, enter that as well.

During the Call

  • Microphone & Camera: Use the controls at the bottom to toggle your audio and video.
  • Screen Sharing: Click the monitor icon to share your screen with other participants.
  • Focus View: Double-click on any participant's video to pin it to the main stage. Double-click again to unpin.
  • Sidebar: Toggle the sidebar using the panel icon to view the chat and participant list.
  • Device Settings: You can change your microphone or camera mid-call from the Participants tab in the sidebar.

Troubleshooting

If you cannot hear others or they cannot hear you, please verify your browser permissions and ensure the correct hardware device is selected.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 9, 2026
Last Updated: July 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CallMee ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our video calling platform.

By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Room Information: Display Name, Room ID, and Room Password (if applicable). We do not require permanent user accounts.
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, operating system, and browser information.
  • Communication Data: Chat messages and file attachments shared during active meetings. This data is ephemeral and not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Audio and Video Data: We transmit audio and video only to provide the video calling service. We do not permanently store your audio or video content.

If recording is enabled, recordings are processed and stored locally on the user's device. We do not have access to these recordings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide video conferencing, chat, and file-sharing services
  • Authenticate users into secure rooms
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and improve service quality

4. Meeting Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (WebRTC DTLS/SRTP for media, TLS/WSS for signaling)
  • Password-protected meetings
  • Peer-to-peer media transmission where possible

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use local storage to remember your preferences, such as selected camera or microphone devices, to enhance your user experience. You can control this through your browser settings.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with law enforcement or regulators only when legally required to do so.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the services. Connection data, chat messages, and files are temporary and discarded when the meeting ends.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. Since we do not store persistent user accounts, data is automatically deleted post-session.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age required by applicable law without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

11. Third-Party Services

Our platform may utilize third-party STUN/TURN servers to facilitate peer-to-peer network connections. These services handle IP routing but do not have access to decrypted media.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

13. Contact Us
